The retirement math no one warns you about

$2,000,000 will pass through your hands — and most keep almost none of it

The average American earns roughly $2M in a lifetime, yet retires with about $60,000 saved. Taxes, interest, and market losses are the reason. Here is what is quietly working against you.

Taxes take 25%–95%

The bulk of a traditional 401(k) or IRA is still taxable in retirement — right when you can least afford to hand it back.

One bad market year

A single downturn near retirement can erase years of growth — and you may not have the time to earn it back.

Interest working against you

Every dollar of interest paid to a lender is a dollar that never compounds for you. Over decades that gap is enormous.

Outliving your savings

With no guaranteed income floor, the fear of running out shapes every decision — and shrinks the retirement you earned.

Products built to confuse

Complex statements and jargon keep you unsure whether your plan is actually working. Clarity is the first thing missing.

Left without a plan

Most savers were never taught how money actually works — so they guess, and hope, instead of following a proven strategy.

What savers ask us first

Is this expensive to set up?+

Your first review is free, and the strategy is funded from savings you're already making — often from the taxes and interest you stop losing. We show you the numbers before you decide anything.

I'm close to retirement — is it too late?+

No. In fact, protecting income from taxes and market losses matters most in the years right before and during retirement. We tailor the plan to your timeline, whether that's five years out or already retired.

Does this really work for my situation?+

We coach individuals, families, and business owners with all kinds of savings — 401(k)s, IRAs, cash, and equity. The review is built around your goals, so you'll see whether it's a fit before moving forward.

What if the market drops?+

That's the point of the strategy. You participate in market gains with only upside potential and no downside losses — a down year doesn't reduce your balance. Your gains lock in and your floor holds.

How do I know I can trust the advice?+

We're educators before advisors. Everything is explained in plain language, you keep control of your money, and you make every decision. Our goal is understanding first — the plan only makes sense once you can see it clearly.

Am I locked into a long contract?+

The coaching relationship is built on fit, not obligation. You start with a free review and move forward only if the strategy clearly serves your retirement. You stay in control the whole way.

How soon will I see results?+

The tax and risk advantages begin as soon as your plan is in place, and clarity comes on day one of your review. Many clients see their projected income rise by 15% to 40% once savings are redirected away from taxes and interest.
