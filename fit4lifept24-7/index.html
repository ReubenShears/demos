Before you book

Questions, answered

How much do sessions cost?
Pricing depends on whether you choose 1:1 studio sessions or online coaching and how often you train. Block bookings are the best value — there's currently 10% off every 12-week block. Book a free consultation and we'll give you an exact quote for your plan.
How quickly will I see results?
Most clients feel stronger and more consistent within the first few weeks, with visible changes building from there. Because your training and nutrition are designed together and adjusted as you go, progress compounds instead of stalling.
I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?
Absolutely. Taj coaches everyone from first-timers to seasoned athletes, and clients consistently describe him as friendly, patient and understanding. Every programme starts from where you are today, not where anyone else is.
Will I actually keep the results?
That's the whole point. Plans are built to be sustainable and matched to your real schedule, so you learn habits you can hold long term — one client went from nearly 23 stone to just over 16 and kept it off.
Can you train around an injury?
Yes. Taj is experienced with injury-related rehab and postural correction — clients recovering from things like ACL and meniscus injuries have trained safely under his care. He's careful, aware, and builds you back at the right pace.
Do I have to commit to a long contract?
No lock-ins. You can train session by session or save with a 12-week block. Everything is built around what works best for you and your schedule, so you choose the commitment that fits.
Where are you based, and do you offer online?
In-person sessions run from our PT studios in Islington N1 and Tower Hamlets E1. If you need more flexibility, the online coaching programme lets you train at home or the gym with weekly check-ins and progress tracking wherever you are.
