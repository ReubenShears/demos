Questions, answered

Everything you're probably wondering

Does working with a broker cost me more?

No. Our consultations are free and the plan price is the same whether you buy through us or direct — you just get an expert shopping the whole market on your side.

I'm busy — how much of my time does this take?

Most clients sort their coverage in a single sitting. Tell us your needs once and we do the comparing — by phone, video, or at our office with free parking.

Do you cover my situation — family, Medicare, or business?

Yes. We serve individuals, families, Medicare clients turning 65, and business owners — Health, Life, Medicare, Long-Term Care, annuities, plus Auto & Home through Kirkendall Insurance.

What if the plan I pick isn't right later?

Your coverage should adjust as life does. We review it with you at renewal and any time your situation changes, and re-shop the market so you're never stuck.

How do I know I can trust you?

We're a licensed, veteran-owned agency operating since 2005 with a real local office — and most of our new clients come from referrals by the ones we already serve.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No lock-in with us. You choose the plan and the carrier; we're your advocate for as long as you want us, at no extra charge.

How fast can I get covered?

Often the same day. Once we find your plan, enrollment is quick — one client had new insurance cards within 48 hours of a life change.
