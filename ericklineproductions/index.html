Questions

Everything you're wondering

What does a Business Film cost?+

It scales to your scope — a single-day shoot is very different from a multi-location documentary. We build a plan around your goals and budget on the call, so you know the number before anything is committed.

I don't have time to make a film. How much do I need to do?+

Very little. It's made for you and with you — we handle scripting, crew, direction and edit. Your main investment is a few hours on camera and a couple of review rounds.

Does this work for my industry?+

Yes. In 30+ years we've told stories across music, sports, nonprofits, healthcare, hospitality and more. Every business has a story worth telling — our job is to find yours and make it cinematic.

What if I don't like the result?+

You won't be surprised at the end. We align on the story and creative direction up front and build in review rounds, so the finished film is one you've shaped every step of the way.

Why work with you over an agency?+

Agencies hand your project to juniors. Here, a proven Producer/Director with 350+ credits and A-list experience is in the chair from the first meeting to final cut. You get the person, not a department.

How long does it take?+

Most Business Films move from kickoff to delivery in a matter of weeks, depending on scope and scheduling. We'll give you a clear timeline on the call so you can plan your launch around it.

What do I actually receive?+

Your finished documentary-style film, plus cutdowns for ads, social reels and press kits — so one production feeds every channel you market on for months.
