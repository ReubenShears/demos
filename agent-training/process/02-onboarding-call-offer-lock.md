# 02 · Onboarding call and offer lock

## Purpose
Extract from the onboarding call the single offer the ads and funnel will sell, in the client's own words, and write it down so creative, copy and funnel all use the same sentence.

## Trigger
The onboarding call has happened and its Fathom recording exists. Funnel copy and creative both wait on this: status updates have read "waiting for fathom recording of the agreed offer to update the copy".

## Inputs
- Fathom recording and transcript of the onboarding call
- Client Data row
- Anything the client dropped in `-main-chat` (case studies, flyers, Miro boards, brand files)

## Steps

1. **Pull the transcript** and read all of it. Do not work from the summary alone.
2. **Write the offer lock** with these fields:
   1. **Front-end offer**: what the ad promises. Optimally's house pattern is a performance offer: "pay per booked job", "pay per shown call", "pay per install", "pay per model-home tour", usually with "no setup fee, no retainer" or "$0 upfront".
   2. **Back-end offer**: what is sold on the call (retainer, programme, price). This never appears in ads.
   3. **ICP in one line**, including the size filter: "US HVAC companies running 3+ trucks, $1M to $4M", "UK residential solar installers, £30k to £80k a month".
   4. **Geography**, with exclusions the client asked for (states, countries, seasons).
   5. **Mechanism**: how the client delivers, in two sentences.
   6. **Proof**: every specific result mentioned, with the client's name for it, the number, and whether it may be named publicly.
   7. **Pains**: 4 to 8, in the ICP's words.
   8. **Who the call is with** and the call name (e.g. "Ad Genius Discovery").
   9. **Qualification questions**: the three questions the funnel will ask and which answers qualify.
   10. **Guarantee** agreed between Optimally and the client, if any (example seen: one closed deal from at least five qualified attended calls at a 20% close rate).
3. **Mark every claim** as `said on call`, `from form`, or `needs client confirmation`. Results quoted from memory on a call must be confirmed in writing before they go in an ad (see step 10).
4. **Collect voice**: 10 to 20 verbatim client phrases. The strongest ads have been "built from her own words, photos and best-performing hooks".
5. **Update Client Data**: Offer, Elevator Pitch, Pain Points, Guarantee, Geographical Constraints, ICP Examples, Case Studies, Sales Call Recordings (the Fathom link).
6. **Post the offer lock** in `#delivery` and tag Ollie and Rey (Funnel Architect).

## Output and handoff
- Offer lock document (one page), posted in `#delivery`
- Updated Client Data row
- Next: steps 03 and 05 run in parallel

## Rules
- One offer per ad set. If the call produced two candidate offers, name both and ask Reuben which leads.
- If the client changes the offer later (it happens: "go back to pay per install"), rewrite the offer lock and tell steps 05, 09 and the funnel owner.
- Never promise results in the offer wording. State how payment works, not what the client will earn.

## Not confirmed
- There is no written onboarding call agenda. The field list above is derived from what later steps consume.
