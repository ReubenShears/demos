# 19 · Client comms and fulfilment status

## Purpose
Make sure every client always knows where things stand, and every internal blocker is visible daily.

## Trigger
Daily (internal status, unanswered-question sweep) and weekly (client update).

## A. Daily fulfilment status (posted in `#delivery`)

Format in use:

```
Update on the funnels and their blockers:

*Almost ready to go live:*
• <Contact> (<Client>) - <blocker>
*Still building:*
• <Contact> (<Client>) - <what is outstanding>
*Funnels with updates:*
• <Contact> (<Client>) - <what changed>
*Went live today:*
• <Contact> (<Client>)
*Already live:*
• ...
```

Other headings used when needed: *Ready to go live*, *Live with new updates*, *Pending changes*, *Waiting for onboarding call*.

Steps:
1. For each client not yet live, find the current blocker from `-main-chat`, `#delivery` and the gates in step 12.
2. Name it in a few words: "waiting for funnel approval", "ads approval + budget", "waiting for DNS set up + new Calendly token", "waiting for updated ads".
3. Tag the person who owns each blocker. Ad sheets are requested from Ollie by client name.
4. Bump clients whose blocker is on their side. Confirm in the update that you did.

## B. Unanswered-question sweep (daily)

1. Read every `-main-chat` channel for client messages with no team reply.
2. Post a list in `#delivery`, tagged to Reuben, one entry per item:
   `<Topic> - <Person> (<Client>)` / time / one-sentence summary / link.
3. Put anything urgent (refund request, cancellation, "turning ads off") under **NEEDS ATTENTION**.
4. Route by type: creative and performance to Ollie; funnel and integrations to Rey; commercial, refunds and guarantees to Reuben; everything else to JJ.

## C. Weekly client update

"From here I'll message you each week with what we're doing and the date for each step. All you'll need to do is approve."

Structure of a good update:
1. **What is running** and since when.
2. **What we are judging it on** (qualified leads and booked calls) and **when** the next read is.
3. **What happens next, whichever way it goes.** Give both branches so the client is never waiting on a result.
4. **What we need from you**, with how long it takes and by when.
5. **Dates** as a short list.

Write plainly. One idea per line. No jargon the client did not use first.

## D. Standard moments
- **Go-live**: "Hello @channel, we're live! The ads are on at `<budget>`/day. 🚀"
- **New batch for review**: see step 10.
- **End of week sign-off** (Friday): let clients know the team is finishing for the week in 30 to 60 minutes, ask for anything they want clarity on now, and say weekend messages are picked up first thing Monday.
- **New point of contact**: introduce yourself in the channel rather than appearing silently; say what the client can count on.
- **Bad news or a frustrated client**: acknowledge specifically what they came for, state what has changed since, say there is a plan, and propose a call the next day. Reuben handles these.

## E. Client questions that need a prepared answer
- "Will you make video ads?" → statics for 7 days, then video based on winners.
- "Can we add a revenue filter to the ad?" → primary text first; explain volume and CPM.
- "Can we target a list we have?" → possible on Meta as a custom audience; raise with Ollie before promising.
- "Does emailing no-shows a booking link hurt tracking?" → no, but those bookings will not carry ad attribution.
- "Can I see the numbers?" → dashboard link.

## Rules
- Reply to clients the same working day.
- Never promise a date another person has to keep without checking with them.
- Refunds, guarantees, cancellations and pricing are Reuben's. Acknowledge, do not negotiate.
