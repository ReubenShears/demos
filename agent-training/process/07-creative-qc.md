# 07 · Creative quality control

## Purpose
Catch everything that would make a client reject an ad, Meta reject an ad, or an ad underperform, before anyone outside sees it.

## Trigger
A batch is produced (step 06), a revision is made (step 11), or client-supplied creative arrives.

## Method
Look at every image at phone size. Check each against the list. Record pass or fail per ad with the reason. Fix or send back, then re-check only what changed.

## Checklist

### Legibility
1. Eyebrow readable at a glance. If you have to look for who the ad is for, it fails.
2. Headline readable in under two seconds on a phone.
3. Supporting copy large enough to read on a phone. Small sub-copy is the single most repeated complaint.
4. Text contrast strong against its background.
5. No garbled, misspelt or half-rendered words (image models do this). Check every word, including small labels inside fake UI.
6. No empty placeholder boxes or leftover template elements.

### Safe zones
7. All copy, logo and proof inside the centre 1:1 square (22% to 78% of height).
8. Nothing important in the bottom 35% for Reels use, or the bottom 20% for Stories.
9. Captions and labels do not collide with graphics.

### Message
10. The ICP is named on the ad, at the right size filter.
11. Offer wording matches the offer lock exactly. Watch for near-misses: "$0 Upfront" versus "$0 Setup Fee"; "restaurants" versus "restaurant groups"; "retainer" wording where the offer has none.
12. Client attribution on proof is explicit and correct (whose result, which client).
13. Numbers match the source: units and period ("$2.5M a year", not "$2.5M").
14. No duplicate concepts within the set.
15. US or UK spelling to match the target market.

### Brand
16. Exact brand colours, client fonts, logo present and not distorted.
17. Semantic colours intact (red for bad, green for good).
18. Imagery fits the niche.

### Policy
19. No third-party logos or platform marks.
20. No named third-party people; no real people without consent.
21. No income promises, no "you will" results claims, no before/after body or personal-attribute targeting ("Are you struggling with debt?").
22. Nothing asserting the viewer's personal characteristics.
23. Client names appear only where the client approved naming; otherwise "one client".

### Items needing client confirmation
24. List every result number, named client, headshot and photo of a person used. These go into the approval message (step 10) as explicit questions.

### Files
25. 1080 x 1920, correct file names, correct count, no stray versions in the delivery folder.

## Output and handoff
- QC table: Ad ID, pass or fail, issue, fix
- A confirmation list for step 10
- If any ad fails: back to step 06. Otherwise: step 08

## Rules
- Regenerate rather than patch when a headline word is garbled.
- A batch that "passed first time" still gets a human-eye check of every word.

## Not confirmed
- No written pass threshold exists. The list above is assembled from every revision recorded in the repo history and Slack.
