# 15 · First 7 days monitoring

## Purpose
Let Meta learn without interference, catch real faults fast, and arrive at day 7 with a clear read on which angles work.

## Trigger
Ads live (step 14).

## The rule
**Leave new ads alone for a full week.** "We leave them alone for a full week so Meta has time to learn." No budget changes, no pausing individual ads, no edits, no new ads in the ad set.

## What to judge on
**Qualified leads and booked calls. Not cost per lead.** A cheap lead that never qualifies is worth nothing; the pixel is trained on qualified leads only, so volume of qualified leads is the signal.

## Daily check (5 minutes per client)

1. **Delivery**: is the ad set spending close to its daily budget? Are all ads Active?
2. **Faults that justify intervening inside the week**:
   - an ad rejected or the account restricted
   - zero spend for 24 hours
   - spend with zero link clicks after a full day (broken creative or audience)
   - clicks with no landing-page views (funnel or domain down)
   - landing-page views with no leads for several days (funnel or tracking broken; run a test lead)
   - leads arriving in `-funnel-notis` with no Ad or Adset (URL parameters lost)
   - payment failure
3. **Funnel-notis**: read the day's leads. Note tier, answers to the three questions, and which ad each came from.
4. **Lead quality**: are qualified leads actually the ICP? Clients will say when they are not ("most booked leads are not 2-location restaurants"). Note it; act at day 7 unless it is extreme.
5. **Log** the day's numbers (step 18 does this automatically; confirm the rows landed).

## Numbers to read (by ad and for the ad set)
- Spend, impressions, CPM
- Link clicks, link CTR
- Landing-page views and load rate (LP views / link clicks)
- Opt-ins and opt-in rate
- Survey completes, qualified count, qualified rate
- Bookings, booking rate, cost per booked call
- Then, as calls happen: shown, closed

## Day 7 review
1. Rank ads by spend. Meta's own allocation shows which angles it found an audience for.
2. For each ad: qualified leads, bookings, cost per booked call.
3. Label each ad: **winner** (spend plus qualified leads or bookings), **spender, no result** (spend, nothing qualified), **starved** (little spend).
4. Write three lines for the client: what won, what did not, what happens next.
5. Take the result to step 16.

## Client communication during the week
- Tell the client the plan and the review date on day 1.
- If they ask for changes mid-week, explain the learning window and offer to queue the change for the review.
- Use the wait productively: script video (step 17) so "nobody sits waiting for results before the next step starts".

## Output and handoff
- Day 7 read: winners, losers, lead-quality notes, recommendation
- Next: step 16

## Not confirmed

- Target cost per booked call by niche.
