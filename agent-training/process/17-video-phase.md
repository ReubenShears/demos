# 17 · Video ad phase

## Purpose
Add video creative once statics have shown which angles work, without making the client wait.

## Trigger
- Day 7 static results are in, or
- statics wore out quickly last time, or
- the client already has strong organic reels and is good on camera (then video can launch with the first statics as a second ad set).

## The plan the client is given
"Make video ads in the meantime, whatever happens with the statics."
- If the statics are working: keep them and **add the videos as a new ad set**. More reach, and fresh creative ready before the statics wear out.
- If they are not: pause the weak ones and make video the main creative.

## Steps

1. **Choose angles** from the static winners. Each winning angle becomes a hook.
2. **Write scripts**
   1. 3 to 4 short hooks for ads.
   2. Optionally one short video per client "pillar" (their main service areas). These double as confirmation-page videos on the funnel, shown according to the lead's answer to a funnel question.
   3. Write in the client's spoken voice, using phrases from their calls.
3. **Send the client a dated plan** in `-main-chat`:
   - scripts to them by a named date
   - they record by a named date: "you record them on your phone. That's fine, it doesn't need to be polished"
   - one session, about 45 to 60 minutes
   - "we do all the editing"
   - go-live date
4. **Collect footage** in a Drive folder. Clients may also send b-roll to splice in; ask them to mark where it goes.
5. **Edit** (Lead Designer)
   1. Vertical 9:16.
   2. Hook in the first two seconds; captions on; key text inside the Reels-safe band.
   3. Review links go through Frame.io (`f.io` links).
   4. Number videos to match the ad sheet (`V1`, `V2` ...).
6. **Alternative source: organic reels.** Cut the client's best-performing reels into ads, with a new call to action on the end.
7. **Ad sheet**: add a video tab or sheet in the same format; `utm_content=Video+Ad`.
8. **Approval** (step 10), then **build as a new ad set** (step 13) named `Video Adset #<n> | ...`.
9. **Minimum spend**: each ad set needs enough daily budget to learn on its own. Confirm the split with the client when adding a second ad set.

## Output and handoff
- Edited videos, ad sheet, approval
- Next: step 13 (new ad set), then step 15

## Rules
- Do not hold statics back waiting for video.
- Do not ask the client for studio-quality footage. Phone footage is the brief.
- Any claim spoken in a video needs the same confirmation as a claim on a static.

## Not confirmed
- Video length, caption style and the edit template.
- The ad set naming for video; `Video Adset #n` is inferred from the static pattern.
- Budget split between static and video ad sets.
