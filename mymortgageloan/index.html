Straight answers

Your questions, answered honestly.

What does it cost to work with you?+

For the vast majority of residential mortgages, my services are free to you — I'm paid by the lender when your mortgage funds. You get expert advice and the whole market shopped at no cost. If a file ever requires a fee, you'll know up front, in writing, before anything moves.

I'm busy — how much time will this take me?+

Your part is about 15 minutes to start and a quick document upload. I handle the lender shopping, negotiation and paperwork. Most clients are pre-approved within 24 to 48 hours of sending their information.

Does this actually work for my situation?+

First-time buyer, self-employed, new to Canada, investor, bruised credit, or renewing — I've placed all of them. With 30+ lenders including credit unions and private options, there's almost always a path. The honest call tells us quickly if there is.

What if you can't beat my bank's rate?+

Then I'll tell you plainly, and you've lost nothing but 15 minutes — plus you'll have a professional second opinion confirming your bank's offer is genuinely good. I'd rather earn a referral by being straight than push a deal that isn't right for you.

Why use a broker instead of my own bank?+

Your bank offers one set of products. A broker makes 30+ lenders compete for your business, which typically means a better rate and terms — at no cost to you. One application, many lenders, a real person managing it all.

Am I locked into anything by applying?+

Not at all. A pre-approval and a conversation carry zero obligation. You only move forward if the numbers are clearly better for you. No pressure, ever — that's how I've built a business on referrals.

How soon can I get approved?+

Once I have your basic information and documents, pre-approval often comes back in as little as 24 hours. If you're house-hunting or up against a renewal deadline, tell me — I'll prioritize your file so timing is never the thing that costs you.
