A fair question

Is this a fit?

Who is this practice for?+

Women who want to understand their health before declining health becomes their new normal. Whether you're navigating metabolism, hormones, gut health, weight, or energy — or simply want to stay healthy for as many years as possible — we build a personalized plan around your goals and your stage of life.

How is this different from my regular primary care?+

Traditional primary care is essential, but visits are short and built around diagnosing disease. My approach takes a broader view — time to understand your history, symptoms, lifestyle, and labs to find what's driving how you feel, then one plan that may include nutrition, movement, sleep, stress, medications, hormone therapy, and targeted testing.

Why is this direct-pay, and do you take insurance?+

Not at this time, and here's the honest reason: insurance is built to cover you once something is already wrong. It was never designed to pay for the time it takes to sit with your full history and connect everything before it becomes a diagnosis. Working directly protects that time. I keep it as affordable as I can, HSA/FSA cards are welcome, and I can provide a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

Is telehealth really enough for this kind of care?+

For most patients, absolutely. This care depends far more on careful listening, understanding your history, and reviewing your lab data than on a traditional office visit. Appointments happen through a secure telehealth platform, so you get personalized care from the comfort of home.

What if it doesn't work for me?+

We work from measurable markers, not guesswork. Your biological age and expanded labs give us a baseline we track over our three months together, so you can see whether things are moving in the right direction — and we adjust the plan around what your body actually shows.

How quickly will I see results?+

Many women feel meaningful shifts within the first weeks as we address the drivers of energy, sleep, and blood sugar. Deeper markers move over the three-month Blueprint as we recheck your labs — real change you can see, not a quick fix that fades.

Do I have to commit long-term to get started?+

No. Start with a complimentary consultation to talk through your goals and see if we're a fit — there's no pressure and no obligation. If another option suits you better, I'll tell you honestly. You can begin with Know Your Number and move up only when you're ready.
