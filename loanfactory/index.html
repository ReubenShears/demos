Does it really cost me anything to check my rate?+

No. Seeing your personalized rate is completely free and there's no obligation. A rate check won't lower your credit score, so there's no downside to finding out where you stand.

How can your rates be lower than my own bank's?+

Your bank offers one set of products. We put your loan in front of 237 lenders who compete for it, our 2,605 loan officers negotiate collectively, and we take a smaller margin. That combination is why our pricing consistently comes in lower.

I'm self-employed — can I still qualify?+

Yes. We offer Bank Statement, Asset-Qualifier, and DSCR loans that use your deposits, savings, or rental income instead of W-2s and tax returns — built specifically for self-employed borrowers and investors.

Not at all. A quote is just information. You're never obligated to proceed, and there's no contract or commitment until you choose the loan that's right for you.
