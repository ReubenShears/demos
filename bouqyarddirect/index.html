Before you book

Your questions, answered

How much do wedding flowers cost?

It depends on your guest count, venue, and vision — which is why we start with a free design consult and give you a clear, itemized quote. No surprise fees for delivery or setup; it's all included up front.

How far in advance should I book?

We take a limited number of weddings per season, so popular dates fill fast. Six to twelve months out is ideal, but reach out anytime — if your date is open, we'll reserve it (and add your free throw bouquet).

Do you deliver and set up at my venue?

Yes — every piece is delivered fresh on the day and set up in place, from bouquets to arches to reception centerpieces. You never have to wrangle flowers on your wedding morning.

Can you match a specific inspiration photo?

Absolutely. Bring your Pinterest board or a saved photo — couples consistently tell us we're the vendor who "immediately saw the vision." We design to your palette and style, then refine it together.

What if it rains or my plans change?

We plan for it. We build a rain plan into every outdoor wedding and stay flexible if dates shift — several couples have rescheduled with us, and we handled it without missing a detail.

Do you do corporate and non-wedding events?

Yes. Alongside weddings we design farm-fresh florals for corporate events, galas, and celebrations across Huntsville and North Alabama — same fresh sourcing, same hands-on setup.

How do I know the flowers will be fresh?

Our blooms are farm-fresh — cut days before your date, not warehoused for weeks. That's why they hold their color in your photos and still look gorgeous when guests text you about them the next morning.
